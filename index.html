How I lead

Habits that kept large purchase systems reliable, and kept teams intact through reorganizations.

  • Catch problems before customers do

    I ran weekly live-site reviews that covered alerted incidents and also the odd data trends that point to a problem. As Executive Incident Manager I drove cross-team incidents from troubleshooting through postmortem repairs.

  • Grow the people doing the work

    I moved engineers into the Senior and Principal bands and into Engineering Lead roles, and kept teams motivated and productive through division restructures.

  • Build with partners, not around them

    Purchase experiences live inside other teams' products, so I worked with Xbox, Windows, Minecraft, Barclays US and an overseas engineering team to make them fit.

  • Treat security and compliance as product work

    I served as Security Champ, kept threat models current and worked through PSD2 and GDPR changes. I held a CISSP certification from 2005-2008 and continue to apply the learning and principles gained from it.
